The working dashboard for From Education to Impact, the ISMMS MD Program Strategic Plan for 2026–2031: its five priorities, goals, Year 1 initiatives and KPIs, with the two initiatives Curriculum Content Gap Closure (G2.2) and the Emerging Healthcare Competency Framework (G3.1), their members, milestones, meetings and materials.
