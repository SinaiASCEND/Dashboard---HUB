Clinical Skills · Access-code dashboard

Three separate dashboards behind one access code. Two cover the Practice of Medicine and PEAKS 1, linked by skill domain: how each class performed in each clinical skill, and the checklists, rubrics and quizzes that produced those scores. Each class runs POM 1 (August) to PEAKS 1 (December of the following year). The third, Clinical Skills Milestones, follows nine clinical skill strands across all three phases, from POM to residency.

What you will find

01
Clinical Skills: Dashboard
Each class's strongest and weakest skill domains across POM 1, 2, 3 and PEAKS 1, with class comparison.
02
Clinical Skills: Analytics
Performance by skill, framework, quartile band or faculty group, with sortable heatmaps.
03
Clinical Skills: Frameworks
The six skill domains mapped to MEPOs, AAMC competencies, Core EPAs and communication functions.
04
Rubrics: Library and items
Every POM checklist, rubric, scoring guide and quiz answer key by class and term, searchable to the single item (ASCEND, RPS, CCM).
05
Rubrics: Crosswalk and Compare
One skill across every rubric that scores it, and any two deliveries of a rubric side by side.
06
Clinical Skills Milestones
Nine clinical skill strands across the three phase gates: the milestone at each gate, the behaviors a rater checks, where it is taught, in-course assessment and the PEAKS gate assessment (PEAKS III is the TTR final assessment).
Who this is for. Clinical Skills: OCA leadership (ASCEND), the EEC, the Pre-Clerkship and Curricular Integration Subcommittees, Clinical Competency Mentors and RPS. Clinical Skills Rubrics: ASCEND, RPS and CCM only, because it holds standardized-patient checklists, faculty-only rubrics and quiz answer keys. Clinical Skills Milestones: every portal code (curriculum content, no student data). All three sit behind the portal's own server-side gate; your Hub code opens what your role allows.
