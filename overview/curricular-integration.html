Where the four Areas of Curricular Concentration (AOCs: Healthcare Delivery Science, Scientific & Scholarly Discovery, Leadership & Professional Identity, Patient-Centered Advocacy), the ten content areas (curricular threads) and The Inquiry (THINQ) Curriculum live across the three phases of ASCEND. Breadth: which modules and clerkships carry each one. Depth: how many sessions and hours, and how Practice of Medicine skills progress. Alignment: which sessions carry objectives, MEPO links and summative assessment. Built from the AOC, thread and THINQ tabs of the master curriculum map; many tags still await director review.
