Support Tools · Access-code dashboard

Four tools for the people who keep ASCEND running, behind one access code. Make a Word draft of meeting minutes from a transcript, build a module evaluation report from a one45 export, take student names out of a file before it goes in a dashboard inbox, or look up how a dashboard job is done. The Office of Curricular Affairs keeps a copy of each meeting transcript; nothing else you upload to these tools is stored.

Open the Support Dashboard →

What you will find

01
Meeting Minutes Generator
Drop in a Zoom or Teams transcript, or paste its text, and get a Word draft of the minutes: overview, decisions, action items with owners, and discussion by topic. The Office of Curricular Affairs keeps a copy of each transcript. Not for EEC, subcommittee or Monday Curricular Affairs minutes: those stay in the Governance dashboard.
02
Program Evaluation Report Generator
Upload a module's one45 evaluation export and get the Module Evaluation Report in Word, with rating charts, last year's ratings and Claude-coded comment themes, or download the latest finished report as a PDF.
03
Remove Student Names
Take names, usernames, IDs and emails out of a gradebook or export inside your browser (nothing is uploaded), with or without a key, and save the DEID copy.
04
How-To Library
Plain-language guides for committee coordinators and dashboard maintainers: the Governance dashboard buttons, meeting files, checklists, and how each dashboard is kept up to date.
Who this is for. Office of Curricular Affairs staff, governance support, and dashboard maintainers.
