Class Schedule · Access-code dashboard

For planning and review by the Office of Curricular Affairs only; not the schedule of record. The class schedule exactly as it stands in the Office of Curricular Affairs' Google Calendar, refreshed automatically every 30 minutes. Two small calendars for the current and next month sit at the top of every view; open on today's sessions with what is next up, see the week as a Monday-to-Friday board, or list every scheduled session grouped by module, by session type (LEC, SGD, CCSG, LAB, PNL, REV, INT and the assessments) or by week.

Every scheduled session from today onward (or the whole calendar), grouped by module, by session type or by week, with each group collapsible. Combine with the module or type filter to see one module's or one type's full schedule.

Cohort, module and type filters
Switch between class calendars, pick one module, choose session types or assessments from a dropdown, show only mandatory sessions, or search any session by name. The two mini-calendars show where the filtered sessions fall.
