Nutrition Milestones

Climb higher.Reach further.Care deeper.
Curriculum Mapping · Access-code dashboard

End-of-phase milestones for nutrition, built on the Milestones Map framework. It takes one competency from the JAMA nutrition competencies for medical students (Eisenberg et al., 2024), #5: assessing a patient's nutritional status with a brief diet history, measurements and lab tests. It follows that competency from the end of Phase 1 to residency readiness, and marks what is already taught and what is proposed.

Milestone statements
What a student must demonstrate at the end of Phase 1 (simulated), Phase 2 (core clerkships) and Phase 3 (Chronic Care Clerkship and Acting Internships), with Miller level, supervision and criterion.

Learning objectives
Six objectives per phase, each tagged to MEPOs and related JAMA competencies, with where it is taught.

Observable behaviors
The checklist a rater uses to see the milestone: eight or nine behaviors per phase.

Instructional methods and assessment methods (with curriculum-map AM codes), each marked existing, expand or new.

Current coverage
The Phase 1 nutrition sessions on the master curriculum map and all 36 JAMA competencies with their coverage.
